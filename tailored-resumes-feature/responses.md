## Personal

### Name an unambiguous empirical belief that you believed within the last 3 years that you now think you were wrong about. Briefly explain why you changed your mind about it.

*Used for: Hadrian (Oct 2026) | Status: **Final***

I used to believe the universe was fully deterministic. I thought that every future event was fixed and that any randomness in the world was just due to a lack of information. During my time at RPI, I had friends who were physics major and they told me about Bell test experiments and how they ruled out local hidden variables which meant that the universe was truly random. This conversation was super fascinating to me and I grew to love this implication because it is a very optimistic view of the world. It is to the view the world that every one of my actions was not predetermined and that I can change my future however I'd like.
